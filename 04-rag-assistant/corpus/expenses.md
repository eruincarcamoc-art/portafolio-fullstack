# Travel and Expense Policy

## Submitting expenses

Expenses must be submitted within 30 days of the purchase through the expense tool. Receipts are mandatory for every expense above $25. Approved claims are reimbursed within 10 business days through the next payroll run. Claims submitted after 30 days may be rejected.

## Meals

While travelling for work, the daily meal allowance is $60 per person. Alcohol is not reimbursable. Team meals during offsites are budgeted separately by the organizer and approved by a director.

## Flights

Employees book economy class for flights shorter than 6 hours. Business class is allowed only for flights longer than 8 hours and requires approval from a VP before booking. Flights must be booked at least 14 days in advance whenever possible to take advantage of lower fares.

## Hotels

Hotel stays are reimbursed up to $220 per night in standard cities and up to $300 per night in high-cost cities such as New York, London and San Francisco. Employees should use the preferred hotel list when available.

## Corporate card

Managers and employees who travel more than four times a year can request a corporate card. Personal purchases on the corporate card must be reported and repaid within 15 days. Lost cards must be blocked immediately through the card provider's app and reported to the finance team.
