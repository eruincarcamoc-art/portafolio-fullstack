# Service Level Agreement

## Availability

The Team plan guarantees 99.9% monthly uptime and the Enterprise plan guarantees 99.99% monthly uptime. Scheduled maintenance does not count as downtime. Uptime is measured by our external monitoring probes and published on the status page.

## Service credits

If monthly uptime falls below the guaranteed level but stays at or above 99%, the customer receives a credit of 10% of the monthly fee. If uptime falls below 99%, the credit is 25% of the monthly fee. Credits must be requested within 30 days of the incident and are applied to the next invoice.

## Support response times

Starter customers receive a first response within 48 hours. Team customers receive a first response within 8 business hours. Enterprise customers have 24/7 support with a first response within 1 hour for critical issues.

## Maintenance windows

Scheduled maintenance takes place on Sundays between 02:00 and 04:00 UTC. Customers are notified at least 7 days in advance by email and on the status page.

## Backups and recovery

Backups are taken every hour and retained for 30 days. The recovery point objective (RPO) is 1 hour and the recovery time objective (RTO) is 4 hours. Restore procedures are tested every quarter.
