# New Hire Onboarding Guide

## Before your first day

Your laptop is shipped to your home address at least 5 days before your start date. Accounts for email, chat and the HR portal are created the day before you start. Please complete the identity verification form so that payroll can be set up.

## First week

On day one you meet your manager and your onboarding buddy, an experienced colleague who answers questions during your first three months. The first week includes security training, an overview of the product, and a first small task that ships to production before Friday.

## Equipment and learning budgets

Every new employee receives a one-time home office equipment budget of $1,500 for a desk, chair or monitor. In addition, every employee has a yearly learning budget of $1,200 for books, courses and conferences. Unused learning budget does not roll over.

## Probation and goals

The probation period lasts 90 days. During that time your manager sets 30-60-90 day goals together with you and holds a check-in every two weeks. At day 90 you receive written feedback and confirmation of your permanent contract.

## Benefits enrollment

Health insurance and retirement plan enrollment must be completed within 30 days of your start date. Coverage starts on the first day of the month following enrollment.
