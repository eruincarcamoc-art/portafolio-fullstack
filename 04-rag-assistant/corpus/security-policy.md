# Information Security Policy

## Passwords and authentication

All employee passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Passwords must not be reused across systems, and we recommend using the company password manager to generate them. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for every internal system, including email, the VPN and the source code platform. Administrators must use hardware security keys instead of authenticator apps.

## Devices

Every laptop must have full-disk encryption enabled (FileVault on macOS, BitLocker on Windows) and must lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. If a device is lost or stolen, the employee must report it to the security team within 1 hour so that it can be remotely wiped. Personal devices may not access production data.

## Phishing and suspicious email

Suspicious messages must be reported with the "Report phishing" button in the mail client. Never forward a suspected phishing email to colleagues. The security team triages every report within one business day and publishes a monthly summary of the campaigns that were blocked.

## Access control

Access follows the principle of least privilege. Managers review their team's access rights every quarter, and access to production systems expires automatically after 90 days unless it is renewed. Contractors receive access only to the systems named in their statement of work.

## Data classification

Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Customer data is always Restricted and may only be stored in approved, encrypted systems. Restricted data must never be copied to personal cloud storage or sent through personal email accounts.
