# Time Off and Leave Policy

## Paid time off (PTO)

Full-time employees accrue 1.75 days of paid time off per month, which adds up to 21 days per year. Accrual starts on the first day of employment and the balance is visible in the HR portal. New employees may use their accrued balance after the first 30 days.

## Carryover

Up to 5 unused days can be carried over into the next calendar year. Any balance above that limit expires on December 31. Carried-over days must be used before March 31.

## Requesting time off

Requests are submitted through the HR portal and approved by your manager. Absences longer than 3 consecutive working days require at least 2 weeks of notice. Short absences of up to 3 days only need 2 working days of notice, subject to team coverage.

## Sick leave

Each employee receives 10 days of paid sick leave per year, separate from PTO. A medical certificate is required only for sick leave longer than 3 consecutive days. Sick days do not carry over.

## Parental leave

Birth and adoptive parents are entitled to 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave, which can be taken in one block or split into two blocks within the first 12 months. Benefits and equity vesting continue during the leave.

## Public holidays

The company observes the public holidays of the country where the employee is based. Employees who work on a public holiday receive a day in lieu within the next 60 days.
