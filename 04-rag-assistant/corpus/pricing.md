# Plans and Pricing

## Plans

The Starter plan costs $29 per month and includes up to 3 seats. The Team plan costs $99 per month and includes up to 15 seats and priority support. The Enterprise plan has custom pricing, unlimited seats, single sign-on and a dedicated account manager.

## Billing

Paying annually gives a 20% discount compared with monthly billing. Invoices are issued on the first day of each billing period and can be paid by card or, for annual Team and Enterprise plans, by bank transfer. Prices are shown excluding taxes.

## Free trial

Every new account gets a 14-day free trial of the Team plan. No credit card is required to start the trial. At the end of the trial the account moves to the Starter plan unless you upgrade.

## Usage overage

Storage above the included allowance is billed at $0.10 per GB per month. Customers receive an email alert when they reach 80% of their included storage.

## Refunds and cancellations

Annual plans can be refunded in full within 30 days of purchase. Monthly plans are not refunded but can be cancelled at any time and remain active until the end of the paid period. Downgrading takes effect at the next billing date.
